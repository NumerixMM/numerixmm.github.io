Understanding Calorie Tracking and Food Logging

The Transform with Science app allows you to set custom daily calorie targets and log your daily food intake to monitor your progress over time.

A Note on Calorie Data and Accuracy

The calorie values provided for each food item are based on standardized, publicly available nutritional databases for general portion sizes. However, food composition, preparation methods, and actual portion sizes can vary significantly.

Example: The app might estimate that a standard serving of "Chicken Biryani" contains 300 kcal, but your specific homemade or restaurant portion—due to differences in ingredients, oil content, rice-to-meat ratios, or serving size—might actually contain 400 kcal (or more).

User Caution and Discretion

Because estimated values are general averages, always exercise caution and personal judgment when logging your meals. We strongly recommend:

  • Cross-referencing app estimates with your actual food, ingredients, and portion sizes.
  • Using a digital kitchen scale whenever possible for more precise tracking.
  • Adjusting your logged entries or custom foods if you know your portion or recipe differs from the standard database entry.
